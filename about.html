ABOUT REJOICE LADIES

Rejoice Ladies is a women's football team created to encourage young women and girls to participate in football. Our goal is to create a positive environment where players can develop their football skills, confidence and teamwork.

Our Community

Rejoice Ladies aims to bring players, coaches and supporters together. We encourage teamwork, discipline and respect while helping young women become confident both on and off the football field.

Boitumelo Joyce Rabale (born 5 August 1996) is a Mosotho professional woman footballer who plays as a forward for SAFA Women's League club Mamelodi Sundowns and captains the Lesotho women's national team.

Rabale was named the 2023 CAF Women's Champions League player of the tournament.

Rabale left Bloemfontein Celtic and was awarded a Kick4Life Student-Athlete Scholarship where she moved to Lewis and Clark Community College.
