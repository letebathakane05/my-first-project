Women's Soccer

Women's soccer is growing around the world. Many talented female players are inspiring young girls to participate in football and follow their dreams.

Lesotho Women's Team

Lesotho's women's national football team is known as Mehalalitoe. The team represents Lesotho in international football competitions.

Boitumelo Rabale

Boitumelo Rabale is one of Lesotho's talented female footballers. She has represented Lesotho and has played football at a high level in South Africa.

Inspiring Young Girls

Football gives young girls an opportunity to develop confidence, teamwork and leadership. Female footballers can inspire the next generation to believe in themselves.

Football in Lesotho

Football is one of the most popular sports in Lesotho. Women's football continues to develop as more girls and women take part in football activities.
